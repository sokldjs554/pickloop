# Fresh size and SKU photo protocol

Use unmodified public photos of real juice packages. Search the original numeric Open Food Facts uploads for the three registered Swedish 1 L products, demo-only 250 mL orange and same-brand size, market and variety alternatives. Supplement small cartons with direct retailer packshots if OFF views have already been used. For optically visible code controls, select up to two additional unregistered juice products with independently verified product labels. Prefer one front and one other side per GTIN, including difficult views. Choose based on source, identity, prior use and manual view inspection before inference. When multiple eligible photos fulfill the same role from the same source, prefer earlier native image IDs. Keep latent package identity even when the panel hides it. Exclude isolated digital art, wrong-product pictures, duplicated compositions and old study images including resizes and apparent crops of the same exposure. Do not treat multiple photographs of one product as independent products.

Group all photos with the same GTIN together across suppliers. A supplier photo repeated at multiple sites is one exposure. Assign ground truth from the product records, GTIN and printed product where visible. Keep the 250 mL orange, any available Swedish 1 L orange and adjacent unregistered challengers in final. Reserve the Swedish 1 L apple and alternate size apple controls for development. Separate any other GTIN by assigning mixed grapefruit and other orange varieties to final and other apple varieties to development. Label evident blur and glare manually, without reference to decoder output. Freeze source/label and blind input files before any classifier is run. No parameter or sample changes using final predictions.

Score photo_name against the three order GTINs, and score confirmed_order_sku separately. A name on a different GTIN is a false identification even within the same flavour and size. Separately score the browser barcode decision against its four-entry registry and report whether a visible code was decoded. Count a hold on a registered item as missed coverage. Always include numerators, denominators and per-photo predictions, including zero-cell strata. No population-wide accuracy estimate.
