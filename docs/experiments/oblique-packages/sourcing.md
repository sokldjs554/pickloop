# Supplemental source review

The new side/reverse controls are original Open Food Facts uploads. They show two distinct physical acquisition settings. One has a hand-held organic orange carton against a bright window. The other shows a Fruit Crush carton against a different background. The organic-orange photos have the same uploader, scene and carton and were uploaded 17 seconds apart. The Fruit Crush image shows a complete ingredients side with the barcode. The adjacent front from the same upload session establishes the fruit mix and packaging. Only source links and hashes are distributed. [Image URLs, original uploader attribution and UTC upload times](supplemental-labels.csv) · [Open Food Facts image terms](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/terms-of-use)

Selection used the original files and the visible panel before recognition. Eligible side and reverse images show the whole or nearly whole same-brand carton. Front-only uploads, tight crops of text, pictures of bottles, and photos with unknown identity were excluded. We inspected available records for eight branded products in the public brand search and for the existing six publisher controls. These convenience samples estimate neither the real-world frequency of difficult viewpoints nor the frequency of similar products. The two organic orange frames share a group even though they show different faces. [Organic orange 1 L](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/product/7310865487490) · [Fruit Crush](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/product/7313619002147)

The screen compared each of the three images against 6,178 historical file records using SHA-256, oriented pixel hashes and paired pHash/dHash on both whole and trimmed images. Neither pair of perceptual hashes was within the threshold of eight bits. A local SIFT screen against 284 related images also ranked potential overlaps. The highest match, 58 inliers between the organic-orange nutrition side and the archived orange frame RR-ecbd85e9ce9de65e, aligns shared printed nutrition text. One photo shows an organic product at a window, the other a regular orange carton held in front of a store shelf. [Pairwise screen summary](supplemental-duplicate-screen.csv) · [Prior screening method](../registered-recall/README.md)

The three registered 1 L GTINs had 12 numeric uploads between them, all of which had appeared in earlier project screening. A separate search found Wolt/Coop records with explicit GTINs. The mixed-juice oblique packshot has the same white flap, cap position, side text, front and crop composition as earlier Dabas, Natmat and Torebrings images. The Wolt apple and orange packshots show straight fronts. Arla's front was available, but 11 attempted alternative GS1-style image names for apple returned HTTP 404. The Handlarn Gunnebo orange product JSON also returned HTTP 404. No additional registered side photograph with verified identity and distinct acquisition survived the review. [Wolt/Coop assortment](https://wolt.com/sv/swe/stockholm/venue/coop-finn-malmgren/items/juice-72) · [Prior publisher images and adjudications](../registered-recall/public-screening.csv)

The supplemental positive denominator is **zero**. The new foil groups can detect some false registered names. A manufacturer's packshot, a crop of an old source, a second frame of the same carton and a synthetic perspective warp cannot establish new consumer-photo recall.
