## Cohort and scope

40 full physical photographs: Banana8, Cucumber8, Pineapple8 (registered broad classes), Strawberry8 UNKNOWN; four unregistered Artichoke and four unregistered Pumpkin lookalike controls. Registration was checked using only the fixed catalogue names, not model outputs/learned vector values. Artichoke's scaled bud was prespecified as a pineapple lookalike; pumpkin's round/ribbed body as a melon lookalike. These are appearance-based controls, not claims of exact commercial SKU identity. No plantain, pear/apple variety, zucchini/cucumber confusion or mandarin/satsuma guess was mapped into a registered class. Green/short bananas with plantain ambiguity were excluded before inference.

Each retained category takes its first eligible photographs in ascending original Open Images test ImageID order, until target count. eligibility-review.json records every source through that category's stopping rank, with semantic exclusions. Source labels were not accepted blindly: cherries/rambutan under Strawberry, zucchini under Cucumber, and pine cones/succulents under Artichoke were rejected by source/pixel review. Prepared dishes, preserves, carved pumpkins, plastic imitations, mixed ambiguous targets and graphical depictions were excluded. A few raw-target photos have minor background items or physical slicing, explicitly recorded; entire source extents are retained.

The new test split is separate from all116 previously visually inspected validation candidates. All40 raw and decoded-pixel hashes are new, and all40 have different original Flickr authors from one another and from the prior16 selected authors. Cross-cohort dHash results are in prior-perceptual-review.json; repeated-image source IDs and originals were also compared. These checks reduce exact/near duplicates, but do not prove that no physical fruit instance was ever photographed by another author. Original images were captured before this evaluation; newly assembled held-out does not mean newly photographed in2026. Foundation encoder pretraining exposure cannot be ruled out.

## Blinding and freeze

blind.csv contains only opaque id/path/sha256. blind/ contains full RGB pixels as PNG generated from a new RGB array, so no EXIF, ICC or textual/source filename metadata is inherited. No crop, resize, rotation, recolouring or augmentation was applied by this collection. CVDF itself delivers at most1024-pixel-long-edge image copies. labels.csv is a separate gold/provenance ledger for join only after predictions are sealed. Generic fruit/cucumber visible in the pixels is normal visual evidence, not metadata leakage. freeze.json binds the two CSV hashes. No frozen files were changed after handoff to the evaluator.

This is a modest independent source test, not a statistically representative market-photo sample or physical pick evaluation. Report known/unknown denominators separately; keep it separate from juice primary, stress and earlier exploratory validation controls.
