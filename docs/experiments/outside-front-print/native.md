# Printed product families in the browser

The candidate adds four supplier references to the reader. Its 737,201-byte gallery loads on demand through the existing OpenCV.js worker. It reports a family when the evident foreground carton has a complete identifying mark, has a supported product title or capacity, and the preceding optical and carton readers have no decision. A family supplies neither a registered name nor an order. Examples in the browser include Original Lemonade and Ginger Shot.

The additional 1.75 L observation has a narrower use. An ambiguous carton comparison still holds the exact identity and the order. A capacity annotation requires the full God Morgon mark, the Smooth Orange title, and evidence for the 1.75 L field on the same foreground face. The two independently projected surfaces must be convex, contain each other's center, overlap at least 75% of the smaller surface, and differ in area by a factor of at most three. If pose extrapolation to the bottom seam drifts, the reader searches within 65% of one text-field height around the predicted position. The full field and each of its five printed glyphs must support the same displacement. Optical confirmation, conflicting barcodes, and other existing identity conflicts retain their precedence.

The family method was developed after inspecting the earlier 16-photo challenge and results of the complete-artwork experiment. In particular, the Norwegian 1.75 L carton was inspected while correcting the browser implementation's sensitivity to resizing. Its result is a targeted retest. The two previously examined Open Food Facts contributors used as positive checks took different photographs from the enrolled manufacturer material. Both photos were available during development. Their confirmed family names provide regression evidence. Neither represents a new prospective success. [Original frozen experiment](README.md) · [Native chooser observations](native-chooser.csv)

## Browser results

Across 124 full-image worker reads (122 distinct original byte hashes), the component confirmed three outside families. All three were previously examined photographs. It made zero false outside calls on 60 registered store originals and no family call on the 14 archived plain-grapefruit controls. None of the 20 freshly sourced, unflagged field or packshot rows produced a family call. Several new review photos come from the same shoot and eight depict multiple products, another brand, or an unidentifiable container. Six additional supplier-lookalike rows and two original screening controls are listed separately. These results give a one-photo targeted improvement in the 16-photo near-miss study and no measured independent fresh-positive gain. The full file chooser confirmed both developmental consumer-photo families and retained correct names for all three selected archived apple, orange and orange/red-grapefruit store targets. It held the exact identity and order on the targeted 1.75 L carton, while describing its out-of-registry capacity. Two independent multiple-package scenes, an unrelated Brämhults bottle and an unseen Orange Mandarin consumer photo received no positive outside annotation. The latter represents a coverage miss. ## Rebuild the four measurements

The browser data file contains ORB keypoints and descriptors plus small quantized high-pass measurements of the registered print. Each supplier original is identified by SHA-256. The Swedish 2025 supplier brochure contains Original Lemonade in PDF object 740 and Ginger Shot in object 806. The Finnish 2021–22 brochure contains Orange Mandarin in object 97. The fourth reference is a Norwegian Arla Smooth Orange 1.75 L packshot. The complete original photographs and PDF documents are available from the recorded sources. This study includes no full supplier or review photographs. The printed trademarks and supplier artwork remain the property of their respective owners. The reviewed sources supply no open license for their original photos; Arla's published website terms reserve image rights and refer to registration for image-bank uses. The photographs contributed to Open Food Facts can carry CC BY-SA terms. They are evaluation inputs and supply no browser template. [Source regions and URLs](family-registrations.json) · [Norwegian product listing](https://www.arla.no/vare-merkevarer/god-morgon-smooth-appelsin-175-liter-899/) · [Arla terms](https://www.arla.com/legal-information/standard-terms-of-website-use/) · [Open Food Facts data and image terms](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/data)

The builder used OpenCV 4.11.0, NumPy 2.2.6 and PyMuPDF 1.26.6. It verifies the two original PDFs, the four extracted or downloaded source images and the expected generated image-measurement file. For an offline build, place the four exact original photo files in a directory under the IDs listed in the registrations and omit the fetch option. The generated byte digest is `c42008bd80f58bdec6a14008cfe9099b6bd0625132a8c2496f975b8aa98268d3`. [Builder](build_browser_gallery.py)

```sh
study=docs/experiments/outside-front-print
```
